### 5. IN / OUT punches and the 4-hour lock

What the code does today (read from `punch/index.html` and `js/api/punch-api.js`): the terminal does NOT ask IN or OUT. It alternates automatically: first punch = `login`, next = `logout`, next = `login`... There is a per-day punch limit setting. Offline, it only looks at punches saved on that device, not the server. There is no minimum gap between punches.

My reading of your rule (please confirm, task 20): after a `login`, ANY punch within 4 hours is rejected with "You have already logged in for the day"; the first punch after 4 hours is the `logout`.

## D. Feedback and suggestions

### G.1 How a punch travels today

1. Terminal: the labor types the ID and presses IN or OUT; the face is compared with THAT labor's saved face (threshold `confidence_threshold`, default 70).
2. Online: `terminal_check_punch` (rules) -> photo upload -> `terminal_record_punch` with **date = `DateUtils.today()` and time = `DateUtils.now()` from the device**. In the database: `_punch_day` decides the shift day (night rule), `_punch_check` applies the IN / OUT rules, the punch goes into `punch_records`, `_recalc_attendance` writes the day into `daily_attendance`.
3. Offline: the punch is kept in the browser and uploaded later through the same function (`SyncManager.syncPunchesTerminal`).
4. Then **three different pieces of code calculate hours and status again, each with its own rules** (root cause of most mismatches, finding 83):

| Rule | Database `_recalc_attendance` -> month screen colours, dashboard counts | `report-api.js` -> daily report, overtime page | `report-api.js` -> 3PL billing | Punch page -> hours line on the month screen |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| Start of the day | first punch of ANY type (an OUT too) | first punch of any type | same as daily | first IN |
| Full day | department Min Hours (default 09:30); shift required hours ignored | same | same | - |
| Half day | fixed 4 h | half of the department full day (4h 45m for 09:30) | same as daily | - |
| Friday | normal day | punched -> P / H / A (with LOP button); not punched -> F | always F, paid, punches ignored | not marked |
| Holiday | normal day | punched -> P / H / A; not punched -> NH | always NH, paid, punches ignored | grey only without a record |
| Admin approval | **overwritten by the next punch of that day** | shows `final_status` when `approved_by` is set (so the overwritten value) | same | separate "approved" flag |

### G.5 Owner decisions (2026-10-19) and what was built (v105, migration 022)

Decisions: (1) labors do not punch between midnight and about 3 a.m.; a night shift is IN in the evening (around 19:00-20:00) and OUT in the morning. (2) **No half day anywhere**: less than the required hours = Absent. Required hours = the shift's required hours, else the department's Min Hours (default 09:30), else Settings. (3) **Friday is always a paid day, punched or not**; the sandwich rule decides only if it is switched on in Settings; "absent" for the sandwich rule = any Absent day (short hours or no punch, not approved). (4) Days approved earlier as half day become Present, fully paid. (5) The rules apply to ALL months (re-running an old month can give different numbers than the copy already sent).

Built:
- `supabase/migrations/022_no_half_day.sql`: status P / A only (database refuses H), first IN to last OUT, required hours from shift / department / Settings, approvals kept on later punches, every day with punches recalculated once (all months), old approved H -> P, other H -> A, waiting LOP requests H -> P; month-screen function sends the days around the month + sandwich switches + joining date; a day's punches listed by time. Tests: `tests/db/no_half_day_before.sql` + `tests/db/no_half_day.test.sql` (17 checks; the whole database suite passes).
- `js/utils/attendance-rules.js` (new): Friday / holiday / sandwich rule, used by the daily report, overtime page, 3PL and the terminal's month screen. Sandwich: a run of Fridays and holidays is Absent when the last working day before it AND the first working day after it are Absent; a run that holds a holiday follows the holiday switch, Fridays only the Friday switch; a day not finished yet (today or later), before joining or after leaving never counts as absent; an administrator can approve such a day (then Present).
- Reports take status and hours from `daily_attendance` (one calculation, in the database) instead of recalculating from punches; they read it page by page. Daily report: H filters, half-day count and the H choice in Approve are gone, a Friday / holiday shows "Friday (paid)" even with punches, approval always gives P, Undo goes back to the day's own status. 3PL: H column gone on screen, Excel and print; paid days = P + F + NH; Excel salary fixed for several months. Dashboard: "Today Half Day" gone. LOP page: half day can no longer be requested. Settings: half-day hours field gone. Departments: hint text.
- Month screen: past Fridays / holidays green (paid) or red (lost by the sandwich rule); today = device date.
- `tests/day-rules.test.js` (11 checks, node only, in CI): Friday with one punch = F, sandwich on / off, holiday block, no H, approved Friday, 3PL = daily report day by day, paid days and salary, reading past 1000 rows. Month-screen colours checked once in a real browser with a stubbed terminal (not kept as a test).
- Not tested: on the live project and real phones. Browser suites (tests/*.test.js against staging) cannot run since staging is gone; `tests/shift-reports.test.js` and `tests/punch-month.test.js` still expect the old behaviour in places.
